Privacy policy

Reclusive Raven tools · last updated 14 September 2026

This covers the personal tools I keep under Reclusive Raven. Flux is the only one that uses data from a Google account, so most of what follows is about Flux. I built these tools for myself and my family and don't offer them to anyone else.

What Flux can see

Only the Google account its owner connects, within the permissions Google's consent screen lists: reading, labelling and sending Gmail, and reading and writing Google Sheets.

How the data is used

  • Flux reads emails from job sites such as LinkedIn and Indeed to pull out job listings and updates on applications. Once it has processed an email, it labels or archives it, depending on the owner's settings.
  • To extract those listings, Flux sends the text of the emails to Anthropic's Claude model, through the Claude Code tool on the same computer. Anthropic handles that text under its own privacy terms.
  • Flux sends email from the account only for things its owner turns on or approves: a digest to themselves, or a job application they have reviewed.
  • It writes the owner's listings to the Google Sheet they connect, and reads status changes back from it.

Sharing

I don't sell this data or pass it to advertisers or data brokers, and I don't use it to train any model. Apart from Google's own APIs and the Claude processing described above, it stays on my computer. I don't look at anyone else's email or listings unless they ask me to help with a problem.

Flux's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Everything Flux keeps, including the tokens Google issues, lives in a database on the computer Flux runs on. There's no server of mine holding a copy.

The data stays until its owner removes their profile in Flux, which deletes everything stored for that account and revokes Flux's access at Google. You can also revoke that access at any time from the third-party connections page of your Google Account.
